Each field has the same name in Salesforce and on the Clay company Audience. Under the name: the Salesforce API name, then the Audience field id. Values land on the Audience first, for RevOps and account-agent QA and fill-rate checks; the Audience fields are mapped to Salesforce in the write-back phase, after testing. # is the data point number on the kickoff mapping. Data point 19, senior leaders, is built but has no Salesforce field yet, so it is listed under extra fields. EMEA has no regional field on the mapping; EMEA headcount appears in the regional summary.

Extra fields we return, beyond the 40 16

Not on Navan's write-back mapping. Each has its own field on the Clay company Audience, next to the mirrored fields. They add the context behind each value: which source won, why we trust it, and what to check. That lets a person on RevOps or an AI agent review a value before it reaches Salesforce.
